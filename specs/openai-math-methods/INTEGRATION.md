## Scientific exclusions

This integration adds no canonical topic, theorem, semantic promotion or
upstream accepted-proof claim. It does not activate the frozen H3 primary,
regenerate historical bridge evidence, run Hermes/OpenGauss or establish
empirical validity. Numerical panels are explanatory calculations of their
declared formulas. New mathematical proof packets remain proposals until their
own declarations, countermodels and source-bound acceptance exist.
